- **Built**: Created `src/lib/game/formulas.ts` exporting pure, unit-testable game loops (base tier XP, capped streak multiplier, daily XP, and exponential saturating resolution mapping). Created `src/lib/data/generateJourney.ts`, a seeded deterministic PRNG generator that builds a 250-day narrative journey matching energy distributions, pillar balance, and act boundaries. It integrates 40 human reflection templates, streak freeze rules (2-freeze capacity with slow recovery), and 8 narrative milestone beats. Added a self-tuning loop that automatically aligns completion rates to land on exactly 98.6% final resolution. Created validation checker `src/lib/data/checkJourney.ts`. Added npm scripts `seed` and `seed:check` to `package.json`. Updated Zustand store in `src/store/useRunwayStore.ts` to manage the persona day (default 250), live AI toggles, session states, and client-generated `liveQuests` map.
- **Decisions**: Implemented binary-search/iterative scale tuning in the generator script to ensure changes to pools or seeds automatically converge on the required 97–99% final Day 250 resolution. Modeled the Duolingo-style streak freeze behavior by regenerating freezes only after 14 consecutive clean days, resetting the counter upon missed days.
